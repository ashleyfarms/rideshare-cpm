Contact FareMax

Questions, bug reports or ideas for FareMax are welcome. Albert reads every message.

How to reach us

Use the contact form at help-pal-apps.com/contact and mention FareMax in your message.

Helpful details to include

  • What you were trying to do.
  • What happened instead.
  • Your phone or computer and browser, for example "iPhone, Safari".

We usually answer within a few days. We never sell your email address; see our Privacy Policy.
