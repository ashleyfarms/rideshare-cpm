About FareMax

FareMax helps rideshare and delivery drivers work out what each mile really costs them, including gas, maintenance, insurance and wear, so they can tell which trips and offers are worth taking.

What you can do

  • Enter your car's gas mileage, fuel price and running costs.
  • See your true cost per mile.
  • Check whether an offer pays enough after costs.
  • Works offline once loaded.

FareMax gives estimates for planning, not tax or financial advice.

Free, supported by ads

FareMax is free to use. There is nothing to buy and no subscription. We keep it free by showing a small number of Google ads. Your entries are stored on your own device unless a feature clearly says otherwise.
